Questions

Where does my resume go?

Only into your own AI account. This site has no backend, no forms, and no analytics.

What should my resume include?

Everything: every role, project, win, and number you might want to use. Long is fine. ResumeMachine picks what's relevant, but it can't add what isn't there.

Will it make things up?

It's instructed not to invent jobs, titles, dates, skills, or numbers, and leaves placeholders like [X%] when it needs a detail. AI still makes mistakes, so read the final version before you send it.

Can I get it as a Word doc or Google Doc?

Yes. Each tailored resume comes back as a Google Doc, or with the quickest way to make one, and it offers a Word version too.

How do I get updates?

Your assistant checks this site for newer instructions. When there's an update, it uses it right away and gives you the new copy to paste into your workspace.

Will it get me past screening software (ATS)?

It uses the posting's wording where it's true for you and keeps formatting plain, which screening software reads best. Nothing is guaranteed, and a referral helps far more than any keyword.

Does it work on free plans?

Usually. If your plan has no Projects or Gems, paste the instructions at the start of each chat instead.
