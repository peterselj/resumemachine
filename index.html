How it works

You set up a permanent workspace once. After that, every job takes about a minute.

  1. Create a workspace in your AI app

    A Project in Claude or ChatGPT, or a Gem in Gemini. It remembers the instructions and your resume.

  2. Add the ResumeMachine instructions and your base resume

    The instructions are a plain text file you can read in full. Your resume goes straight into your own AI account.

  3. Paste a job posting

    Open a new chat in the workspace, paste the full posting, and review what comes back.

Manual setup

Prefer to do it yourself? Get the instructions file, then follow the steps for your assistant.

Download instructions
  1. Go to claude.ai and open Projects in the sidebar, then Create project. Name it "ResumeMachine".
  2. Open the project's instructions and paste the ResumeMachine instructions.
  3. Upload your base resume to the project's files.
  4. Start a new chat in the project and paste a job posting.
  1. Go to chatgpt.com and choose New project in the sidebar. Name it "ResumeMachine".
  2. Open the project settings and paste the ResumeMachine instructions into Instructions.
  3. Add your base resume under the project's Files.
  4. Start a new chat in the project and paste a job posting.

Tips for better results

  • Make your base resume a master copy. Long is fine. Include every role, project, and win you might want to use. ResumeMachine picks what's relevant; it can't add what isn't there.
  • Include numbers. Team sizes, budgets, percentages, timeframes. These make the strongest bullets.
  • Paste the full posting text, not just a link. Many job sites block AI assistants from reading them.
  • Answer its questions. When it asks for a detail, your answer makes the resume better. Add it to your base resume too.
  • Always read the final version. It's your name on it. Check every claim before you send it.
